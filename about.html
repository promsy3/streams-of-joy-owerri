About Streams of Joy Owerri — Testimony House

Streams of Joy Owerri (affectionately known as Testimony House) is a vibrant expression of Streams of Joy International. We are a supernatural movement anchored on the prophetic mandate that "What God Cannot Do Does Not Exist!"

At Testimony House, our atmosphere is continuously charged with the raw power of God, resulting in an endless influx of miracles, healings, and supernatural turnarounds. We are passionately dedicated to raising a generation of Christ-centered believers who are deeply rooted in prayer, active in faith, and fully equipped to walk in their divine destinies.

Church Leadership

Pastor Jerry Eze

Lead Pastor & Visionary, Streams of Joy International

Pastor Jerry Eze is the Apostolic Lead of Streams of Joy International and the visionary behind the global prayer movement, NSPPD. Driven by a profound burden for souls and a relentless passion to see God’s people walk in liberation, his ministry continues to impact millions around the globe through aggressive prophetic prayers, signs, and deep manifestations of the Holy Spirit.

Pastor Uguru Eme Uguru

Resident Pastor, Streams of Joy Owerri

Pastor Uguru serves dynamically as the Resident Pastor of Testimony House, Owerri. Working under the direct mantle and vision of the Lead Pastor, Pastor Uguru shepherds the Owerri local expression with immense grace, deep scriptural insight, and a fierce prayer fire. Known for his exceptional leadership and radical commitment to the flock, his ministry has made Owerri a true oasis of testimonies.
