🤝 What to Expect

The Atmosphere

Expect high-energy, prophetic praise, deep worship, and fervent prayer led by our resident ministers and the strong anointing of our resident pastor, Pastor Uguru.

Dress Code

Come as you are! You will see everything from traditional corporate attire to smart casual wear. We focus on your heart encounter, not your outfit.

Parking

Secure parking is monitored by our traffic and security teams around the Okigwe Road roundabout zone. Protip: Arrive 20 minutes early for smooth parking!

Childcare

Our dedicated Streams Kids ministers provide a fun, safe, and heavily monitored environment where your children can learn about God while you focus on the service.

📖 Our Beliefs

Streams of Joy International is a Bible-believing, Word-centered Pentecostal ministry.
